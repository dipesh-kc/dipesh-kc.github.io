01 / ABOUT

Engineer · Researcher · Educator

Dipesh K.C. is a mechanical engineer, energy systems researcher, and faculty lecturer at Paschimanchal Campus and Pokhara University. He received his B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from Tribhuvan University and M.Eng. in Planning and Operation of Energy Systems from Kathmandu University, graduating Cum Laude. He was also a DAAD-funded research exchange scholar at TH Köln, Germany, and has published research in Clean Energy and Energy for Sustainable Development. Alongside research, he is actively involved in engineering education, teaching Fluid Mechanics, Fluid Machines, Mechanics of Solids, Turbomachines, and Engineering Drawing. His professional experience also includes energy auditing, engineering review, and technical analysis, enabling him to bridge theoretical knowledge with practical engineering applications. His long-term goal is to contribute to innovative, reliable, and sustainable energy solutions for developing countries through interdisciplinary research and engineering education.
